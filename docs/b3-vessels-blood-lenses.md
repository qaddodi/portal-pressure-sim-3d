# B3: vessels, blood and lenses

What the 2D figure draws, in 3D: every vessel by engine edge ID, the moving blood, twelve colour lenses with a key, and the Blood menu's figure options.

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `src/scene/vessels/` | `network.ts` (55 vessels, `NETWORK`, presence rules), `vessel-object.ts` (a `CelObject` with the lens- and blood-aware shader), `shaders.ts`, `presence-track.ts` (draw on / retract), `paths.copied.ts` (2D courses, generated) |
| `src/scene/lenses/` | `model.ts` (the twelve lenses' colours per edge from an engine frame), `field.ts` (two lens states on the GPU, 420 ms crossfade), `legend.ts` + `Legend.tsx`, `blood-model.ts` (origin and oxygen), copied tokens, words and velocity model |
| `src/scene/blood/` | `options.ts` (Blood menu choices, `useBlood`, kept in localStorage), `marks.ts` (chevrons by flow-mark option, key chevrons, presence) |

## How to use it
- `sceneApi.setLens('velocity')` colours the veins (`'neutral'` for the plain anatomy); `useLens` holds the choice for menus and the key. Arteries keep their own colour under every lens, as in 2D.
- `sceneApi.setBlood({ chevrons, keyChevrons, collaterals }, presenting)`; `BLOOD_MENU` has the menu's rows and words. `chips` and `labels` are for the tags and cards.
- `<Legend />` (`src/scene/lenses/Legend.tsx`) draws the key; the shell mounts it and sets `--lg-title`, `--lg-num`, `--lg-width`.
- `sceneApi.loadHealthy()` fetches the healthy patient at rest once the engine is ready (Stage does this); the change, congestion, velocity and direction lenses compare to it.

## Rules kept
- A vessel is named by its engine edge ID everywhere (the artist's real anatomy replaces geometry, not names). Surgical shunts and stents are B5's: build them as `NetVessel` and `VesselObject`.
- Copied 2D data is generated: `node scripts/copy-b3-data.mjs --from <2D repo> [--check]`. `PPS_2D_REPO=<path> npm test` proves the copies are current.
- Nothing is drawn per frame by the CPU: lens colours are two 208 x 2 float textures per lens set, recomputed at most every 90 ms from the engine's frames; the crossfade, chevrons, smoke and ghost threads are shader work. A vessel that comes or goes draws on and retracts along its flow (`presence-track.ts`).

## Draw calls
One draw per vessel: the geometry holds the tube twice (`withHull`, attribute `hull` 0 for the body, 1 for the ink's hull) and the vessel shader draws the body's front faces and the hull's back faces (pushed out in screen pixels) in the same mesh. 55 vessels plus the organs are 86 meshes (about 52 to 70 of them visible for a patient), under the spec's 100. `CelObject.ink` stays as the highlight's handle on the merged material's uniforms.

## 20 Nov: smooth vessel joins (Settings > Figure depth, `useSettings.smoothJoins`, on by default)
A deliberate step beyond the 2D figure, all behind the switch (`vessels/join-mode.ts`; off, the tubes are byte for byte the 2D rule's: same vertices, same positions). `vessels/smooth-joins.ts` holds the rules; `shape.ts`
reads them (`coursesOf`, `fitEnds`), `tubes.ts` builds them (`clamp`, `point` on a `Course`), the vertex shader eases live widths (`mate0`, `mate1`, `swellOf`), and the lens texture carries two more rows (`lensSmooth`).
- **Run-on pairs are one tube**: the two end rings share centre and radius (a pair of different caliber, a vein into a liver bed, narrows the wide one to the thin one's width; nearly equal ones ease to their mean), no cap, and the shader eases the two vessels' live engine widths to one mean over the same 30 % (`joinSwell`), so no seam or step shows when the engine widens one and not the other.
- **Branch ends** move onto the trunk's centreline (when within 1.6 radii: the 2D art's own larger misses are left) and their collar and cap are held inside it (`BURY`), so there is no gap and no stub out of the far wall.
- **Free ends** with no alpha fade of their own taper to a point (`TIP_K`); every free end the network has today carries a 2D alpha fade, which stays the rule.
- **The cava and the SVC end in the right atrium**: one slope to the atrium's back wall (`NODE_RISE`, z 0.06; the atrium stands 0.02 to 0.20) instead of lying 0.3 behind it.
- **Pressure colour along a vessel** passes through the ramp's own colours (`grad` rows 2 and 3, a third and two thirds along) instead of a blend of the ends.
- Toggling rewrites every tube in place (`CelScene.useSmoothJoins`, `VesselObject.reshape`); `tubularOf` always counts on the 2D courses so the vertex counts never depend on the switch. The one-outline stencil (`hullMat`) is unchanged and shared by every vessel (`tests/b3-smooth-joins.test.ts`).
- Not done: merging the static vessels into few draw calls (each vessel keeps its own lens texel, presence, picking and flow stream uniforms).

## Blood-cell particles (20 Nov, depth upgrade 5; `src/scene/blood/cells/`)
An additional flow mark, off by default (Settings > Figure depth > Blood streaks, `useSettings.particles`); the chevrons stay the default and are untouched.
- **One draw call**: a single `THREE.Points`, one vertex a cell, up to 2400 cells on a desktop and 1200 on a phone (`CELL_CAP`, `particleBudget` in `core.ts`: cells per vessel proportional to its design flow, at least 2 and at most what its tube holds, the rest shared on). Built the first time it is switched on (nothing in the scene before then); faded out it is `visible = false` and asks for no frame.
- **Positioned on the GPU** (`shader.ts`): each cell carries (edge, phase, thinning order). Its place is its phase carried by the chevrons' own stream distance (`streamTex.x`, from the engine's flow: forward runs on, reversed runs back, none stands), wrapped at the ends and faded there; how many of a vessel's cells show is the engine's flow (`vdata.g`) against the vessel's design flow, thinned in a van der Corput order so any share is evenly spread; its size and width follow the engine's diameter (`vdata.b`) as the vessel's own shader does.
- **Course** (`courseTex`, 64 points a vessel) is read from the tubes' own vertices (`ringCentres`) and read again when a vessel reshapes (a collateral coiling); a second small texture (`edgeTex`) carries each vessel's length, width rule, taper, veil, draw-on and tip fade, polled from the vessel's uniforms and uploaded only when one changed.
- **Look**: a cel disc with a thin ink ring (the vessels' casing ink), one shadow tone lower right, a faint dimple; the fill is the vessel's colour under the lens (same lens textures) lightened. Drawn in the vessels' pass with depth test on, on the tube's centre line moved toward the camera along the view ray by the tube's radius, so it shows on the lumen inside the silhouette and a vessel really in front still hides it. Vessels behind the organs keep the veil's 0.4 (the cava 0.2).
- **Hidden** in the Circuit, the lobule and the sinusoid, with the body's dive fade, and with Reduce Motion on (not drawn at all, rather than a still scatter).
- Tests: `tests/b3-cells.test.ts` (budget, wrap, reversal, thinning, rows, the switch, the program); the program is registered in `src/debug/gl-registry.ts` and compiled by `tests/glsl-compile.test.ts`.

## Zigzag cava and fork wedges (20 Nov, Mohammad's phone screenshot)

- **The zigzag IVC was depth.** `NODE_Z` (network.ts) was the mean of the tiers at a node, so the back-tier cava was pulled toward the camera at the renal-vein junction (and by the shunts' ends, which sit on the cava's nodes in tier "over"), and the 14 degree view turned each bulge into a sideways jog. At a node of three or more, the widest main vessels now keep their tier and the narrower ones ease to it; shunts and collaterals never set a node. The hepatic junction (`RISE_NODES`: IVCS) keeps the mean on purpose: the cava rises there to meet the hepatic veins and runs on to the atrium on one slope (atlas-camera.test, b3-smooth-joins.test).
- **Black wedges at forks were heads carried through a turn.** A chained link draws the head on both tubes; where the courses turn, each tube cuts it at its end and the next draws it again turned. `chain.ts` `linkStraightness` eases a link's end weights to 0 between 11 and 35 degrees of turn (the child's entry end, and the parent's exit end by its straightest child). The pattern (A, B, root) is unchanged, so spacing does not move. Note `par` and `root` from the 2D chain are edge keys, not item indexes.

- **Key colours and head ink (20 Nov, external review):** the lens key (bar, dots, the menu swatch, the action card's strip) is read through `figureHex` (`cel-ink.ts`, the vessels' own saturation and brightness caps) after the ramp is read, so a key never shows a colour the vessels do not wear (`tests/legend-figure-colours.test.ts`). A vessel's flow-head ink (dark or cream) is latched with hysteresis and eased per vessel (`lenses/chevron-ink.ts`, fifth row of the lens colour texture), with a short eased band where the vessel has none (`tests/chevron-ink.test.ts`).

## 20 Nov: junction skins (one welded mesh per junction; `src/scene/vessels/skin/`, spec `rebuild-3d/junction-skin-spec.md`)
- At a junction the arm tubes are cut at a seam ring (the tube's own ring vertices) and one **skin** mesh starts bit for bit on those vertices: the smooth-min union of the arms' tapered capsules (`SKIN_K` 0.8 x the narrowest arm radius). The GPU blends live width (swell), colour, fade, highlight, lens and the stenosis waist (`armNarrow`) per arm weight; the ink is the outline of the union in screen px. The tube's `skinCut` uniform discards what lies past the cut (a screen-door dither in the Circuit); marks fade over `MARK_FADE_R` radii before the seam. Two skins that share a vessel are merged (`A+B`) and the shared vessel is hidden (`skinCut(2,-1)`).
- `SkinSet` (`skin-set.ts`) builds them in `CelScene` (`refreshSkins()`, only with Smooth vessel joins on); a skinned end drops its mate and bury (`applyMates`). Collaterals, shunts, feeders, SVC_E, SIN_L and SIN_R keep the fork method. A junction that cannot be built (`SkinBuildError`) keeps today's join and warns once; a skin program that fails on a device draws plain through the shader guard (material `skin`).
- **Flow marks cross the junction** (Mohammad, 20 Nov): the skin draws the tubes' dust and chevrons itself, once per arm, with the tube's own code (ONE shared GLSL pair in `vessels/glsl-shared.ts`, `flowDust` and `flowHeads` = `FLOW_MARKS_GLSL`; the tube's THE DUST block and the skin's per-arm loop both just call them) on that arm's arc carried on past its seam (`armArcOf`: the arc at the seam, which way it grows toward the node, the length, the radius; the stream chain's A and B come along by the arm's lens index), blended by the arm weights. On a seam ring one weight is 1, so the pixel is the tube's. The tubes draw their marks right up to the seam (no fade). `tests/skin-marks.test.ts`.
- **`?joins=`**: `mesh2` (the default since 20 Nov, every junction in `inventory.ts`), `mesh` (phase 1: the confluence only), `old` (today's fork joins, no skins). **To revert:** change `JOINS_DEFAULT` in `skin/joins-mode.ts` to `'old'` (one line, its own commit); nothing else was removed.
- Tests: `tests/skin-*.test.ts` (tube cut, twins of the live fade/highlight, seam agreement, scene wiring); the old-method assertions in `b3-smooth-joins` / `vessel-crossings` build `new CelScene({ joins: 'old' })`.
