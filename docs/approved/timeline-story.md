# Approved: the patient's timeline (History as the story of the course)

**Reference:** `/mnt/project-files/mocks/patient-timeline/` (the mock `index.html`, the comparison sheets `renders/sheet-*.png`, the design note `README.md`, the build plan and pre-mortem `plan.md`). Mohammad picked **A** on 26 Nov: the History panel becomes the timeline, oldest first, chapter by chapter, with a strip of the course above it. The list may scroll inside its card on Mac and iPad. Times are day numbers only. The chapter names were approved as drafted in `plan.md`.

## Rules (built so far)
- **Going back keeps the course (step 1).** Going back to a moment ("Go back here", "Show on the figure") keeps the days after it in the day record as the future, as History keeps its later entries; Redo comes back to the whole course. They go when something new is done at that moment (a change, an action, a jump: `Timeline.truncateFuture` calls `branched`, the feed's `dropFuture`), or when the patient moves on to another day. What the restored model re-raises on the moment's own day (a bleed still in shock) is the moment's state, not news, and does not end the future (`Timeline.addEvents`).
- **Every moment a history headlines can be shown (step 1).** The generator keeps the model at the end of each day the story headlines (`timeline/story.ts isKeyEntry`: a course step, a change, an event that is dangerous, critical or a bleed brought under control), on the last line of that day. For a bleed that is after the band. These moments are the past (`Entry.past`): they can be shown on the figure, but Undo and Redo walk only the changes made on this page, and the last line "Day N, today" is where Redo comes back to.
- **The story (step 2, `timeline/story.ts storyOf`).** One card a day for a key day; the days between fold into a quiet run, and the same event again in a run is said once ("Ascites forming, 4 times"). A quiet run never crosses a chapter start or the moment the model is at. A day split by that moment is two cards, the past and the future.
- **A card's numbers** (PPG, HVPG, ascites, shunt; at most three, only those that moved more than the model's noise) come from the series the Over time lanes draw (`bigtime/record.ts valueAt`, `facts.ts before`), so a card and the chart never disagree. An action (a band, a tap) reads before and right after on its day; a procedure reads 8 days later, but never on or past the day of the next change; a medicine's start and an event read the day. A change that moved none of them reads the day.
- **Chapters.** Authored for the 12 histories (anchored to the course's step ids, never the engine's days). Any other patient gets derived ones: "Before treatment" (or "On diuretics" for a patient who comes on one), a chapter at the first start of each medicine ("On carvedilol") and at each procedure ("After the TIPS"). A derived chapter under 3 days joins its neighbour; no treatment, or no days run, is one unnamed chapter.
- **"No carvedilol"** in a change's label is said as "Carvedilol stopped" under the change; "+2 more" stays whole.
- **History keeps its last 300 entries**: the start stays, and the story says the earlier moments were not kept.
- **A tap or a fluid is a step of the day record** (`feed.markStep`, `send.ts STEPS`), as a treatment is: the calmed ascites lane drops at the tap instead of smoothing it away (found by the story; the Over time chart had the same smoothing).
- **A move to a moment is a switch.** The figure's numbers roll to the moment's as they do when a patient is opened (`switchBegins`, `switchLands` on the restored state's first frame).

## The panel (step 3, `history/HistoryList.tsx`, `timeline/strip.ts`, `timeline/story-view.ts`)
- History's panel is the timeline (no second list): oldest first, chapter headings that stick (solid, the card's own surface, no blur), the strip above. Mac and iPad on its side: History's place at the right, about 460 px wide and as tall as the room; iPad and iPhone upright: the phone sheet from the foot; iPhone on its side: the one column at the left (38 %, 290 to 360 px), where the strip scrolls away with the list.
- The strip is one reading (PPG, HVPG, ascites or shunt; a gradient not yet measured is not offered) along the paced axis of Over time, the chapters as faint bands, a line at each key moment, the medicine bars, and the days after the figure's moment veiled. It is a frame for the chart GPU, made again only when the story or the reading changes.
- Scrolling moves only the playhead (a transform on the one clock) to the card under the chapter heading (an IntersectionObserver: no scroll handler, no layout read). The figure moves only on a card's "Show on the figure", which restores that day's end through the switch. On an upright iPad or iPhone the sheet closes so the figure shows; beside the figure it stays open.
- The panel opens on the moment the figure is at, after the fonts are in, scrolled once with no glide. The moments ahead of it are dimmed under "Undone: Redo brings these back".
- No round mark beside the words: the rail beside a key day takes the day's colour.
- **A deliberate difference from the 2D app**: 2D's History lists every entry newest first in a small popover. The words (Go back here, Compare with now, the empty note) are 2D's; the order, the grouping and the strip are this design's (Mohammad, 26 Nov).

## Known difference
- A restored reading can print 0.1 apart from the day record when it sat on a rounding edge: the engine's readings dead band (`holdShown`) starts over on a restore. The fix belongs in hales-engine (keep the dead band in the snapshot).
